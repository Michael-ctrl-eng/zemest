What are tokens?

Tokens are the units used by the AI model to process text. Every chat message and website crawl consumes tokens. Chat tokens = customer conversations. Crawl/Training tokens = product extraction from websites. Free tier models have rate limits. For production, consider a paid model.

Business Settings

Delivery Charges

Set default delivery charges. These are used by the AI chatbot and for order calculation. Individual products can override these if they have a delivery_charge attribute.

How it works: AI tells customers the delivery charge based on their governorate. If order total exceeds "Free Delivery Above", delivery is free. For heavy/special products, add delivery_charge attribute on the product to override.

Mobile Wallet Numbers (Vodafone Cash / InstaPay / Fawry)

Add your mobile wallet numbers with account type. The AI chatbot will instruct customers correctly based on account type: Personal = Send Money, Merchant = Payment, Agent = Cash Out.

Order Placement API (Optional)
